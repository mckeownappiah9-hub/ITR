Flagship project: Computational Systems for Decentralized Bio-Energy

A Python-based biodigester simulation, built as a proof of concept for decentralized, automated waste management. The project grew out of firsthand experience of inadequate sanitation infrastructure.

Waste inOrganic waste enters the digester pit.
Biogas outGas travels through piping to school kitchen burners.
Slurry outThe bio-slurry byproduct is projected as fertilizer.

The model works out frictional pressure loss along the pipe that carries gas from the digester pit to the burners. It uses the Darcy–Weisbach equation for pressure drop and Hagen–Poiseuille flow for the laminar, low-velocity regime. It also projects the bio-slurry from anaerobic digestion as a fertilizer that could earn revenue for the school.

More about our Founder and President

Mckeown Oduro Appiah founded ITR to apply simulation and systems modeling to the sanitation, water and health infrastructure he saw lacking around him in Ghana. As a student at Presec Legon, he was selected as a UN Millennium Fellow, won gold at the International STEM Olympiad and the Kangaroo Math competition, took silver at the Singapore and Asian Schools Math Olympiad, and earned an award at the International Quantum Mechanics Challenge.

He is also passionate about soul winning. As a revivalist, he became a Pentecost Young Missionary and did missionary work around the Greater Accra Region. He was Prayer Secretary of PENSA Presec and a member of the Presec Legon Sharks Quiz team, and he serves as Ministry to Teens Coordinator at the Church of Pentecost, Emmanuel Assembly. He was also on Presec’s second-place robotics team at Airtad 2026, founded the Youth Invasion NGO, and co-founded Benda Tutorials.
